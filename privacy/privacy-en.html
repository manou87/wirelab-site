Privacy Policy — WireLab

Last updated: October 12, 2026
App: WireLab
Publisher / developer: SwissDZ (Electro DZ)

WireLab does not create an account. WireLab does not collect personal data on a SwissDZ server. Diagrams stay on your device, unless you share them yourself (file, email, and so on).

1. Who we are

This policy covers only the mobile app WireLab Pro, an electrical panel wiring simulator for training.

Privacy / support contact:
wirelab@gmx.net

2. Data the app does not access or collect

WireLab does not access, collect, use, or share:

  • identity, email address, phone number, or user account
  • location, contacts, photos, messages, microphone, camera
  • purchase history (no in-app purchases in this version)
  • advertising IDs, ad tracking, third-party analytics
  • health data or financial data

No advertising. No mandatory network connection to use local wiring.

3. What stays on your device

When you save a diagram, the file stays on your device, in the place you choose. SwissDZ does not receive that file automatically.

Local preferences (language, theme) may be stored on the device only for the app to work. They are not sent to SwissDZ.

4. Sharing with third parties

WireLab does not share any personal data with third parties, because SwissDZ collects no personal data through the app.

If you export or share a file yourself, that sharing is under your control (another app, email, personal cloud).

5. Retention and deletion

Because no account is created and no data is stored on a SwissDZ server, there is no user database for us to delete.

To remove what is on your device: delete the diagram files in your storage, then uninstall WireLab. Local preferences go away with the uninstall.

If you wrote to us by email, you can ask us to erase that correspondence at wirelab@gmx.net.

6. Security

Diagrams and preferences stay local. We do not host WireLab user data on a server. Use an up-to-date device and, if you share a file, a channel you control.

7. Children

WireLab is not intended for children under 13 and is not directed at that audience. It is not “made for kids” in the store sense.

8. Android permissions (Google Play)

The Android app WireLab Pro (com.wirelab.pro) declares the following permissions. They are for technical app operation; they are not used to collect personal data for advertising or profiling.

  • android.permission.INTERNET — network access (load content / services needed to run the app, e.g. sign-in if you connect)
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — read files on the device (open / import a diagram you choose)
  • android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — write files on the device (save / export a diagram)
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — haptic feedback for some UI gestures
  • android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — draw over other apps (dev runtime / technical overlays; not for ads)
  • com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE — Google Play license check (installation legitimacy from Play Store)
  • com.wirelab.pro.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION — internal Android permission (non-exported dynamic receivers); no access to your personal data

Total: 7 permissions, matching the Google Play listing. None of these permissions means automatic sharing of personal data with SwissDZ.

9. App stores (Apple / Google)

This page is the privacy URL to enter in App Store Connect and Google Play Console. It must stay consistent with:

  • App Store: App Privacy questionnaire (“Data Not Collected” if nothing is collected)
  • Google Play: Data safety section (“no data collected / no data shared” if nothing is collected or shared)

Apple and Google also require a link to this policy inside the app (About / settings), in addition to the store URL.

10. Changes

If practices change (account, analytics, purchases), this page will be updated before or with the relevant version, and the store declarations will be aligned.
